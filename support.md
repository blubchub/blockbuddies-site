# Buddy Pop support

Buddy Pop is a tap-to-pop puzzle game for iPhone, with a cosy farm to come home to. Tap a group of two or more matching
buddies and they pop. Bigger groups make specials, chains make cascades, and a rare pet can burst out of the blocks.

**Contact:** [lagroue@gmail.com](mailto:lagroue@gmail.com)

## How your progress is saved

- Your progress is saved on your iPhone as you play. There is no save button to press.
- The save lives in the app's private storage on this iPhone, with a backup copy next to it.
- Your save is included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), like most app data.
- There is no iCloud sync in this version. Your progress does not move to another iPhone. If you change phones, your progress
  stays on the old one unless you restore a backup made by your iPhone.

## Erasing your progress

Settings → **Erase All Progress** → confirm. This deletes your save and its backup from this iPhone, including crystals you
bought, and the game starts over as a fresh install. It cannot be undone. Scores already posted to Game Center stay with Apple.

## Game Center (optional)

- You can play the whole game without signing in to Game Center.
- Signing in lets you post scores to leaderboards and earn achievements. The game shows your nickname in Settings.
- Scores are posted only after your iPhone has replayed your moves and got the same score.
- Game Center needs an internet connection. If you are offline, your results wait on your phone and are sent later.
  Up to 50 pending results are kept.
- Game Center is Apple's service. Its privacy terms are at [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy).

## Pet odds

Every pet's chance is published in the app. Open it in either of two ways:

- Settings → **Pet odds**
- The farm's bottom tray → **Pets** (paw icon) → **Odds**

The page explains the odds and the bad-luck protection. Each pet is found in play; money never changes the odds.

## Crystals and crystal packs

- Crystals unlock a pet you have already found. Your first pet is free.
- You earn crystals by growing the seed packets you win in levels on your farm.
- You can also buy crystal packs in the Shop. A pack always gives the same number of crystals; nothing random is sold.
- Bought crystals are kept in your save on this iPhone, and in your iPhone's own backups. There is no iCloud sync, so they do not
  move to another iPhone, and deleting the app or Erase All Progress removes them.
- If a purchase did not arrive, open the game again with an internet connection: a purchase that was not added yet is added at
  launch. For refunds and billing, use Apple's [Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- Purchases follow your iPhone's Screen Time and Ask to Buy settings. A purchase waiting for a parent's approval is added once it
  is approved.

Link text used for this: **See the published pet odds**.

## Known limits in this version

- **iPhone only.** There is no Apple Watch app and no Mac version yet.
- **No iCloud sync.** Progress stays on the iPhone it was made on.
- **Game Center needs the internet.** Offline play is fully supported; only leaderboards and achievements wait.
- **One daily level.** The daily puzzle is the same for everyone and changes every day at 00:00 UTC.
- **Crop-ready alerts are off by default.** Turn them on in Settings → Reminders, or say yes when the game asks after your
  second planting. At most one alert a day, made on your iPhone.
- **No ads.**

## Something not working?

Please email [lagroue@gmail.com](mailto:lagroue@gmail.com) and include:

1. Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About and Software Update).
2. The app version (Buddy Pop → Settings → Version).
3. What you were doing when it happened, and what you expected.
4. A screenshot or screen recording, if you can. Please do not include anything personal you do not want to share.

We reply as soon as we can. Please do not send your Game Center password or any payment details; we will never ask for them.

## Still stuck?

If the game stops responding, close it from the app switcher and open it again. If the problem keeps coming back, email us with
the details above.
