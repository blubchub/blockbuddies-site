# Pop Meadow privacy policy

**Effective date:** 9 November 2026
**Developer:** Harold Lagroue
**Contact:** [lagroue@gmail.com](mailto:lagroue@gmail.com)

This policy covers Pop Meadow for iPhone. It is written for players, in plain words.

## The short version

- Pop Meadow collects no data about you.
- There are no accounts, no ads, no trackers and no analytics.
- You can buy crystal packs in the game. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.
- Your game is saved on your iPhone. The game works offline.
- Crop-ready alerts are off unless you turn them on.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The game does not send data about you, your play or your device to us or to anyone else. We run no server for player data. This matches the "Data Not Collected" label on the App Store page.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code, and it does not ask for permission to track you.

## What is stored on your iPhone

Everything the game remembers stays on your iPhone, in the app's private storage:

- **Your save:** your levels, stars and best scores, coins and crystals, your farm and where you put things on it, your pets, your settings, and a list of the crystal pack purchases already added to your game (Apple's transaction numbers, so a purchase is never added twice). Plus a backup copy of the same save, kept on the same phone.
- **An in-progress pet attempt:** a short file that keeps a board you are part way through, so a pet found mid-board is not lost if the app closes.
- **A random install identifier:** a random number, stored in your iPhone's Keychain for this device only. It is not linked to you and it does not leave your phone.
- **A random number used for pet drops on this phone** (the game's dice), and, rarely, a set-aside copy of a damaged save file so nothing is lost. Neither leaves your phone.
- **A small queue of Game Center submissions** (only if you use Game Center): results waiting to be sent to Apple. The queue holds up to 50 replays and waits on your phone until it can be sent.
- **A few first-run memories:** for example, that you have already seen the farm introduction. These are simple on/off flags in the app's preferences.

Your save is part of your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup, or a backup made on your computer), the same as most app data. Those backups are made by Apple's system, not by the game.

## Game Center (optional)

Game Center is Apple's service. You can play the whole game without signing in.

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple handles your Game Center identity, your nickname, and the scores and achievements you post. That is governed by Apple's privacy policy at [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy).

- The game shows your Game Center nickname on screen, in Settings. It does not save the nickname in your save and does not send it anywhere of its own.
- A score is posted only after your iPhone has replayed your recorded moves and gets the same score. Scores you post are held by Apple, and the game cannot delete them.
- Game Center needs an internet connection. Until then, your results wait on your phone (see above).

## Ads, tracking and purchases

- **Ads:** none. The game has no advertising code switched on.
- **Tracking:** none. We do not track you, and no tracking domains are contacted.
- **Purchases:** the game sells crystal packs (in-app purchases). Crystals unlock pets you have already found in play; you can also earn them free by growing seeds on your farm. Nothing random is sold: a pack always gives the same number of crystals. Apple's App Store handles the payment and your Apple Account; we receive no payment or account details. The game checks each purchase on your iPhone and adds the crystals to your save.
- **Bought crystals live in your save on this iPhone** (and in your iPhone's own backups). There is no iCloud sync in this version. If you delete the app or use Erase all progress, bought crystals are erased too. For a problem with a purchase, use Apple's [Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Notifications

Off unless you turn them on. The game can send one local alert when your farm's crops are ready, at most one a day. It asks for permission only when you say yes to them in the game or turn on Reminders in Settings. The alerts are made on your device; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You can turn them off in Settings at any time.

## Children

Pop Meadow is rated 4+. We do not collect data from children, or from anyone else. There are no accounts, chat or ads. In-app purchases follow the iPhone's own Screen Time and Ask to Buy settings.

## Your choices

- **Erase all progress:** Settings → Erase All Progress. This deletes your save and its backup from this iPhone, including crystals you bought, plus any results still waiting for Game Center. The random install number stays. It cannot be undone. Scores already posted to Game Center stay with Apple.
- **Delete the app:** removes the app and its files from the iPhone; a random install number stays in this iPhone's Keychain (it is not linked to you and never leaves the phone). Scores already posted to Game Center stay with Apple.
- **Game Center:** you can stay signed out, or sign out in the iPhone's Settings. Playing does not need it.

## Future versions

iCloud sync is not in this version. If it is added later, we will update this policy before that version is released. It will say what is synced, and how to turn it off.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top.
